#Create an Agile Story or Feature

###Story in Jira
```text
Can you help me write an Agile Scrum story for <FILL IN DETAILS>
Using the Jira field "Description",
include a "User story" section in the format of:
"As a developer, I need to... So I can...", a context section (a brief description of the intent of the work and additional context to why it's being pursued).
Using the Jira field "Acceptance Criteria", include a Deliverables section and an Acceptance Criteria section.
```

###Feature in Jira
```text
Can you help me write an agile scrum feature in Jira to

Include a benefits hypothesis section, a description section that includes a user story subsection in the format of:
"As a cloud stakeholder, I want to... So I can..." The description section should also include a problem definition subsection,
a technical context subsection with technical considerations, details, and requirements relevant to the body of work.
The description section should also include a forecasted cost subsection, a communication strategy subsection, a prerequisite and dependency subsection,
a risks subsection that includes the risk of not doing this, and a references subsection.
Include an Acceptance Criteria section with a deliverables subsection and an AC subsection.

Could you also classify the feature using one of these categories?
(1) New Market/Product, (2) Grow/Protect Base, (3) KTLO, (4) Tech Debt, (5) R&D.

And identify a "3Ms Investment" category as
(1) Market: items in your roadmap that bring new, highly impactful and valuable functionality. This is either solving new problems in your product space or drastically improving the way a problem is solved.
(2) Maturity: items in your roadmap that are maturing existing functionality. This is continuously expanding how you fulfill the vision and adapting to evolving needs of your market space.
(3) Maintenance: items in your roadmap that keep your product stable, resilient, and reliable. This might be bug fixes, technology debt, embedded technology currency, etc. Fixes are easier for customers to understand, but you can still make a compelling case to the customer of why technology debt needs to be addressed. After all, maintaining too many versions is costly and doesn't give the last and greatest features of the newest technology.
'''
